---
title: What happens if my building receives a B2 rating?
answer: A B2 rating means combustible cladding has been identified and
  remediation works are required. Your property is likely to be unmortgageable
  until works are completed. The next steps involve obtaining a remediation
  specification, identifying funding through the Building Safety Fund or
  Cladding Safety Scheme, completing the works and then commissioning a new EWS1
  assessment to obtain a satisfactory rating.
