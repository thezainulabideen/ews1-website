---
title: Which buildings need an EWS1 assessment?
answer: Buildings of any height with ACM, MCM or HPL cladding panels require an
  EWS1 form. Buildings over six storeys with any cladding or curtain wall
  glazing also require one. Buildings of five or six storeys may require one if
  cladding covers a significant portion of the elevation. Buildings of four
  storeys or fewer rarely require an EWS1 form.
order: 3
active: true
---
