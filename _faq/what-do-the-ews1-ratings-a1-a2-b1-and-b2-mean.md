---
title: What do the EWS1 ratings A1, A2, B1 and B2 mean?
answer: A1 means no cladding or non-combustible cladding is present. A2 means
  cladding is present but the fire risk is sufficiently low that no remediation
  is needed. B1 means combustible cladding is present but the risk is assessed
  as low and remediation is not required. B2 means combustible cladding is
  present and remediation works are required before the property can be
  mortgaged.
order: 4
active: true
