---
title: Do I need an EWS1 form to sell my flat?
answer: You may need one if your building has cladding or combustible balconies
  and your buyer is using a mortgage. If the lender requires an EWS1 form and
  one is not available, the sale is likely to fall through. Cash buyers are not
  subject to lender requirements and may proceed without one.
