---
title: How long is an EWS1 form valid for?
answer: EWS1 forms were originally given a five-year lifespan. However, UK
  Finance and RICS confirmed in 2025 that lenders will not require wholesale
  reviews of EWS1 forms simply because they are more than five years old.
  Existing valid certificates can continue to be used in mortgage applications
  beyond five years, subject to individual lender policy.
