---
title: Who pays for the EWS1 assessment?
answer: The building owner or freeholder is typically responsible for
  commissioning and paying for an EWS1 assessment. If you are a leaseholder and
  your building owner is not acting, you should contact your managing agent or
  freeholder in writing to request one. Under the Building Safety Act 2022,
  qualifying leaseholders have strong legal protections against being charged
  for cladding remediation costs.
