---
title: What is an EWS1 form?
answer: An EWS1 (External Wall System) form is a standardised document completed
  by a qualified fire engineer or building surveyor. It assesses the fire safety
  of a residential building's external wall system, including cladding,
  insulation and balconies, and is used by mortgage lenders to decide whether to
  lend on a property.
